**Q: I already post on MercadoAutos and OLX — why do I need AutoVault on top of that?**

**Q: How long does it actually take to get my storefront live?**
A: The average new dealer publishes their first listing in under 5 minutes. A full storefront with 10 vehicles, photos, and specs typically takes under an hour. There's no approval process, no waiting period — you publish and it's live immediately.

**Q: Can I manage AutoVault from my phone while I'm on the lot?**
A: Yes — AutoVault is designed mobile-first. You can upload vehicle photos directly from your camera roll, update prices, mark cars sold, and respond to leads all from your phone. No laptop required for daily operations.

**Q: What happens to my listings if I downgrade or cancel?**
A: Your data is always yours. If you downgrade from Dealer to Starter, your oldest listings beyond the 10-listing limit are moved to draft (hidden from public, not deleted). If you cancel, you have a 30-day window to export all your listings and lead data before the account closes.

**Q: Do buyers need to create an account to contact me through AutoVault?**
A: No. Buyers never need to register. They browse your storefront, click 'I'm Interested' or 'Contact Dealer,' fill out a simple form or tap the WhatsApp button — and the inquiry lands in your dashboard instantly. Zero friction for buyers means more leads for you.

**Q: Can I accept payments or financing through AutoVault?**
A: The current version focuses on lead generation and inventory management — buyers reach out through the platform and the deal closes through your own process. The Pro Lot plan includes a financing calculator badge on listings (monthly payment estimator) to accelerate purchase intent. Full payment processing is on our Q3 roadmap.

**Q: Is my storefront and data secure?**
A: Yes. AutoVault is hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure (AWS) with SSL encryption on all pages, daily backups, and role-based access control for multi-user Pro Lot accounts. We never sell your data or your buyer lead information to third parties.

**Q: What kind of support do you offer if I get stuck?**
A: Starter plan users get email support with a 24-hour response SLA plus a full help center with video walkthroughs. Dealer plan users get priority email support (4-hour response). Pro Lot users get a dedicated onboarding call, priority support, and direct WhatsApp access to our team during business hours.
