## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Independent Auto Dealers & Car Lot Owners in Latin America / US Hispanic Market**

Small-to-mid-size used and new car dealership owners or independent sellers who currently rely on WhatsApp, word-of-mouth, or fragmented classifieds to move inventory. They need a professional online presence that lets buyers browse, filter, and inquire on vehicles without the seller having to manage chaos across multiple platforms. They are operationally savvy but not technically skilled enough to build or maintain a custom website.

**Demographics:** 1–20 employee car dealerships or independent sellers; owners, sales managers, or lot operators; used/new auto sales industry; primarily Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, USA (Hispanic market); annual revenue $200K–$5M USD

**Pain points:**
- Inventory lives in WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets, or physical notebooks — no single source of truth
- No professional storefront: buyers can't browse, filter, or compare cars without calling the seller directly
- Leads are lost because there's no structured inquiry or follow-up system
- Posting to multiple classifieds (Marketplace, OLX, Craigslist) is manual, repetitive, and inconsistent

## 8. FAQ

**Q: Do I need to know how to code or build websites to use AutoVault?**
A: Absolutely not. AutoVault is designed specifically for dealers and sellers, not developers. You create an account, add your vehicles with photos and details using a simple form, and your public showroom is live automatically. The entire setup takes under 30 minutes with zero technical knowledge required.

**Q: How is AutoVault different from just posting on Facebook Marketplace or OLX?**
A: Marketplaces are crowded, and your listing competes against hundreds of others the moment you post. AutoVault gives you your own branded showroom — a dedicated URL for your dealership where buyers see only your inventory. You also get lead capture, analytics, and a management dashboard that classifieds will never offer. Many dealers use AutoVault alongside classifieds and simply include their showroom link in every post.

**Q: Can I manage multiple vehicles and update prices or photos easily?**
A: Yes. Your dealer dashboard gives you full control over every listing. You can update price, swap photos, change status from 'Available' to 'Sold,' or remove a listing entirely in seconds. There's no approval process or waiting period — changes go live immediately.

**Q: What happens when a buyer submits an inquiry through my showroom?**
A: You get notified instantly via email and, on the Professional and Dealer Pro plans, via SMS as well. The inquiry includes the buyer's name, contact information, and the specific vehicle they're asking about — so you can follow up in context without any back-and-forth to figure out which car they meant.

**Q: Is my showroom visible to buyers without them needing to create an account?**
A: Yes. Your showroom is a fully public webpage. Buyers can browse all your listings, view photos, see pricing and details, and submit an inquiry without registering or logging in. The less friction for your buyer, the more inquiries you receive.

**Q: Can I use AutoVault if I only have a few cars to sell right now?**
A: Absolutely — our free plan supports up to 10 active listings with no time limit. Many sellers start with 2–3 vehicles and grow from there. You only need to upgrade when you want unlimited listings, advanced analytics, or a custom URL.

**Q: Is AutoVault available in Spanish?**
A: Yes. AutoVault supports both English and Spanish interfaces. Your seller dashboard and your buyer-facing showroom can both be set to Spanish, making it fully usable for dealers and buyers across Latin America and the US Hispanic market.

**Q: What does it cost after the free trial, and can I cancel anytime?**
A: The Starter plan is free forever with up to 10 listings — no trial expiration. If you upgrade to Professional ($49/month) or Dealer Pro ($149/month), you are billed monthly with no long-term contract. You can cancel, downgrade, or change plans at any time from your account settings, and you will not be charged again after cancellation.
